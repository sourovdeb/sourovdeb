# Sourov Deb

English language teacher and independent researcher based in La Réunion, France. My work covers ELT methodology, vocabulary retention and spaced retrieval, hospitality English, and open, portfolio-based teacher training, drawing on more than 18 years of international management experience in Australia and France. I also write essays on philosophy and mental health, memoir, and short fiction.

I use GitHub for teaching materials, tools, and the revision history of my writing.
