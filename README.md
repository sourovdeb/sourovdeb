# Sourov Deb

English teacher (CELTA), independent ELT researcher, and writer based in La Réunion, France. My work covers ELT methodology, vocabulary retention and spaced retrieval, hospitality English, and open, portfolio-based teacher training, drawing on more than 18 years of international management experience in Australia and France. I also write essays on philosophy and mental health, memoir, and short fiction.

I use GitHub for open teaching materials, a public portfolio, and the revision history of my writing.

## Public work

### ELT and teaching

- [free_education](https://github.com/sourovdeb/free_education) — free, open English-teaching and teacher-training resources: CELTA-style micro-lessons, ELT365, interactive lessons, and literacy packs.
